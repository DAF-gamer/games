Midnight to 6 AM at Late Nite Mart.

Something is wrong in town tonight. Some of your customers aren't who they say they are. They look human, but their IDs never quite add up.

Check every customer's ID before you ring them up. Refuse the sale if:

    You work locked in the clerk's room. Customers pay at the bulletproof window: IDs, cash and change go through the tray under the glass. The glass stops bullets too, so to use the pistol you have to go out through the door at the back of your room.

    Run IDs through the records computer on the counter, or pick up the aura tablet next to it and point it at a customer: real people glow a calm blue-green, lookalikes flicker red. Its battery only holds 3 scans and gets one back every hour, so save it for the customers you're unsure about. There's also a pistol under the counter. A lookalike you shoot falls apart: bag the pieces (trash bags are behind the counter), dump the bag out back, and mop up the blood before customers see it. Shoot a real person and it's over.

    Shelves run out as customers buy things. Use the ordering computer in your room to buy boxes from the wholesaler with the store's money. The truck drops them in the stockroom out back half an hour later. Carry a box to its shelf to restock it. Customers who can't find what they want leave unhappy.

    Refuse a real customer and they'll complain to your manager. Let three lookalikes through and… you don't want to find out.

    Make it to 6 AM, then walk to the bus stop and catch the bus home.
